The Pomodoro Technique is a simple way to manage your time. You pick one task, set a timer for 25 minutes and work only on that task, with no phone and no switching tabs, until the timer rings. Then you take a short 5-minute break. Each 25-minute work block is called a pomodoro. After four pomodoros in a row, you take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes, then start the cycle again.
